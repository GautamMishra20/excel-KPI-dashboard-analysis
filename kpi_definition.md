# KPI Definitions – Excel Business Performance Dashboard

This document explains the Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) used in the Excel dashboard and their business relevance.

---

## 1. Total Sales
**Definition:**  
Sum of the total billed amount before discounts.

**Formula Logic:**  
SUM(Total Sales)

**Business Purpose:**  
Measures overall revenue generation and business scale.

---

## 2. Net Sales
**Definition:**  
Total Sales after deducting discounts.

**Formula Logic:**  
Total Sales − Discount Amount

**Business Purpose:**  
Represents actual realized revenue and is the primary metric for performance tracking.

---

## 3. Discount Amount
**Definition:**  
Total value of discounts applied to sales transactions.

**Formula Logic:**  
SUM(Discount Amount)

**Business Purpose:**  
Helps evaluate discount strategy and its impact on profitability.

---

## 4. Profit Amount
**Definition:**  
Net profit generated after deducting costs from net sales.

**Formula Logic:**  
Net Sales − Cost

**Business Purpose:**  
Indicates financial performance and operational efficiency.

---

## 5. Profit Margin (%)
**Definition:**  
Percentage of profit earned on net sales.

**Formula:**  
(Profit Amount / Net Sales) × 100

**Business Purpose:**  
Used to assess pricing effectiveness and cost control.

---

## 6. Quantity Sold
**Definition:**  
Total number of units sold.

**Formula Logic:**  
SUM(Quantity)

**Business Purpose:**  
Measures product demand and sales volume.

---

## 7. Average Discount (%)
**Definition:**  
Average percentage of discount applied across orders.

**Formula:**  
AVERAGE(Discount %)

**Business Purpose:**  
Helps analyze promotional effectiveness and discount dependency.

---

## 8. Average Profit Margin (%)
**Definition:**  
Average profitability percentage across products or categories.

**Formula:**  
AVERAGE(Profit Margin %)

**Business Purpose:**  
Used for comparative margin analysis across categories and regions.

---

## 9. Sales by Product Category
**Definition:**  
Net sales grouped by major product categories such as:
- Beauty
- Clothing
- Electronics
- Home Decor

**Business Purpose:**  
Identifies top-performing and underperforming product segments.

---

## 10. Sales by Product Sub-Category
**Definition:**  
Net sales at detailed product level (e.g., Laptop, Mobile, Sofa).

**Business Purpose:**  
Supports inventory planning and product-level decision-making.

---

## 11. Sales by Region
**Definition:**  
Net sales and profit distributed across regions:
- Central
- East
- North
- South
- West

**Business Purpose:**  
Helps identify high-performing and low-performing regions.

---

## 12. Sales by City
**Definition:**  
Net sales and profit grouped by city.

**Business Purpose:**  
Supports regional marketing and operational strategy.

---

## 13. Sales Channel Performance
**Definition:**  
Comparison of Online vs Offline sales.

**Business Purpose:**  
Evaluates channel efficiency and customer purchasing behavior.

---

## 14. Payment Mode Analysis
**Definition:**  
Sales distribution across payment methods:
- Card
- COD
- UPI
- Wallet

**Business Purpose:**  
Supports payment optimization and customer preference analysis.

---

## 15. Delivery Status Analysis
**Definition:**  
Sales grouped by order delivery status:
- Delivered
- In-Transit
- Returned
- Cancelled

**Business Purpose:**  
Helps monitor logistics performance and return/cancellation issues.

---

## Summary
The KPIs collectively enable:
- End-to-end sales performance monitoring
- Profitability analysis
- Operational efficiency evaluation
- Business-driven decision support
