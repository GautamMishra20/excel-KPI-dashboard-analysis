## Business Insights from the Dashboard

1. Net sales provide a more accurate view of business performance than total sales.
2. High sales volume does not always translate into high profit margins.
3. Certain regions contribute higher revenue but show lower profitability.
4. Discount levels have a visible impact on overall profit margin.
5. Online and offline channels contribute equally to revenue, indicating balanced channel performance.
6. Returned and cancelled orders impact net sales and should be monitored closely.
